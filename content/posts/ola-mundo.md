this is a running list of my favorite papers in law, political science and economics, in the order I came across them :)

last updated on oct 08, 2026

Wang, Daniel Wei Liang. 2024. "O conceito de teoria e a sua função em quatro tipos de pesquisa em Direito" [The Concept and the Function of Theory in Four Types of Legal Research]. Revista Direito e Práxis 15 (3): e73590, 1–25. https://doi.org/10.1590/2179-8966/2023/73590.
↳ the paper I reference every time I'm discussing what it means to be a legal scholar.

Limongi, Fernando, Juliana de Souza Oliveira, and Stefanie Tomé Schmitt. 2019. "Sufrágio universal, mas... só para homens. O voto feminino no Brasil" [Universal Suffrage, but... Only for Men: Women's Vote in Brazil]. Revista de Sociologia e Política 27 (70): 1–22. https://doi.org/10.1590/1678-987319277003.
↳ the paper that opened my eyes to the gap between what the law says and what it actually does.

Fujiwara, Thomas. 2015. "Voting Technology, Political Responsiveness, and Infant Health: Evidence from Brazil." Econometrica 83 (2): 423–464. https://doi.org/10.3982/ECTA11520.
↳ the paper that made me fall in love with econometrics.

Costa, Francisco, Angelo Marcantonio Junior, and Rudi Rocha. 2019. "Stop Suffering! Economic Downturns and Pentecostal Upsurge." FGV EPGE Economics Working Papers 815. https://ideas.repec.org/p/fgv/epgewp/815.html
↳ the paper I come back to when I'm thinking about current Brazilian politics.
